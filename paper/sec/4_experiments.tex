\subsection{Selection Headroom (Diagnostic)}
\label{sec:exp:headroom}
To quantify how much of the v2 gap is attributable to \emph{selection} rather
than \emph{generation}, we dump all $N$ candidates per frame and, offline,
measure the per-candidate mean and the oracle ceiling (best-of-$N$ under the
corrected single-stage EPDMS). We stress that the ceiling uses the evaluation
metric to pick and is therefore an \emph{upper bound only}, not a reportable
result; it motivates the deployable surrogate selector of
