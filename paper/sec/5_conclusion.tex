\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented YouDrive, which treats driving style as an explicit, user-steerable
axis and generates trajectories with a style-conditioned flow-matching planner,
decoupling ``how to drive'' from ``drive safely.'' On NAVSIM, YouDrive spans a
controllable style range while retaining strong closed-loop safety.
