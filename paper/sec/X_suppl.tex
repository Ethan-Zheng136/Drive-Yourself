\section{Inference-Time Cost}
\label{sec:suppl:latency}
YouDrive plans in two stages per frame: (i) flow-matching \emph{generation} of
$N$ candidate trajectories using $K$ integration steps, and (ii)
\emph{selection} of one candidate with a simulation-in-the-loop, PDM-style
scorer that consumes only planning-time information (map, perception, agent
forecasts). NAVSIM is not a real-time closed-loop benchmark and imposes no
latency constraint; we nonetheless report per-frame latency for completeness and
to characterize deployability.
